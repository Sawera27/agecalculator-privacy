Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Age Calculator ("the app") is designed to work completely on your device.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information stored on your device

If you enter a date of birth, the app saves it only in the app's private storage on your device so you do not have to type it again. This information never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by tapping the clear button next to the date of birth field, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions. It does not access the internet, your contacts, your location, your photos, your camera, your microphone or any other personal data.

Sharing

If you tap the share button, the app hands a short text message with your calculated age to the Android share sheet. You choose where it goes. The app itself does not send it anywhere.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
